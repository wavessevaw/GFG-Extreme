## What's New in 3.4.2

- **In-game Benchmark:** Hold the HUD for 0.8 s to start a native-FPS/TDP sweep; Apply or Discard the result in-game. No Dash button.
- **Mode-aware tuning:** Separate performance targets for Quality, Balanced, Battery and Autopilot.
- **Benchmark HUD:** Centered panel showing FPS, CPU/GPU load, APU power and temperature.
- **Kedavra Focus:** Independent of performance mode, with live processing status in Graphics.
- **Reliability:** Improved shutdown, power-setting restoration and coordination with the standalone Kedavra plugin.
- **Engine:** Bundled GSF Kedavra 1.6.2.
