## GFG Extreme 1.0.3

**See what GFG saves you: live, and after every game.**

### New
- **Saving, live.** Under *TDP now* the home screen shows how far below your own limit the game is running, for example **"Saving · 11 W under your 20 W limit"**. The reference is the TDP limit your Deck had before GFG took over, i.e. what the game would have been allowed without it.
- **Last session.** When a game you played with GFG closes, the home screen keeps a summary until the next one: how long you played, frames on screen and real frames on average, average TDP against your limit, and the watts saved on average. Loading screens and menus (no frames from the game) are left out of the averages, and sessions shorter than 30 seconds are not counted.
- The live session numbers are also part of the status (`session`) and of recorded logs.

Everything from 1.0.2 is included.

### Install
Download `GFG-Extreme-v1_0_3.zip` below and install it through Decky Loader (*Install from zip*) over your current version; settings, profiles and game memory are kept.
