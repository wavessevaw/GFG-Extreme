# GFG Extreme 3.0.7

Panel fixes from the first look at 3.0.6 on the Deck.

> Tested in CI and in the sandbox. **Not yet checked on a Steam Deck.**

- **Setup is never silent.** Reinstall, Remove, Reboot, Take over, checks, report, engine log
  and GPU benchmark show a Steam toast when they start and when they end (errors included),
  and a line with the result on their own card.
- **Compact Mode buttons.** Smaller icons, one-line subtitles; Steam's large line height no
  longer blows them up to three lines.
- **Roomier header.** The GFG Extreme logo and the Steam Deck OLED chip no longer touch.
- The "waiting for a game" text no longer runs two sentences together; ring digits keep their
  place under Steam's line height.
- The "Independence" name is gone from the panel and the releases.

Everything from 3.0.6 is included (same KEDAVRA engine, c9e49fd).
